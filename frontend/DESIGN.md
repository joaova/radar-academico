## Brand & Style

The design system embodies international curiosity, institutional trust, and youthful ambition. Geared toward university students, researchers, and global exchange coordinators, the visual tone balances the rigor of academic eligibility with the excitement of international discovery.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Soft Minimalism** with **Aero-Dynamic Travel Aesthetics**:
- **Generous Whitespace:** Canvas layouts prioritize room to breathe, reducing cognitive load when evaluating complex visa rules, credit transfers, and scholarship stipends.
- **Micro-elevation & Diffusion:** Flat, ultra-clean surfaces layered over crisp off-white tones, replacing harsh dark drop shadows with ambient, tinted sky-blue and slate diffusions.
- **Dynamic Exploration Cues:** Floating metadata chips, rounded passports/boarding-pass metaphors, and high-visibility status indicators that evoke movement, air travel, and global horizons.

## Colors

The palette establishes an open, luminous sky atmosphere anchored by structural slates and energized by high-visibility accents:

- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Deep Sky):** Directs the primary navigation, institutional affiliations, link states, and foundational brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#F43F5E` - Sunset Coral):** Reserved strictly for critical conversion hooks: "Apply Now", urgent scholarship deadlines, and primary intent triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Vitality):** Signals high academic compatibility scores (85%+ match), verified prerequisite compliance, and approved documentation statuses.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Research grants, postgraduate mobility, and academic consortium badges.
  - **Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Impending deadlines, document warnings, and pending validation states.
- **Neutral Framework:** Crisp, ultra-clean base layers (`#FFFFFF` base surfaces against `#F8FAFC` page canvases and `#F1F5F9` structural dividers), framed by `#0F172A` for high-contrast primary typography and `#64748B` for supporting metadata.

## Typography

The type scale uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to ensure geometric clarity, welcoming humanistic warmth, and high legibility in dense academic tables.

- **Numerics & Data-Points:** Deadlines, monetary amounts, and ECTS credits are set in semi-bold and bold weights with tabular alignment where applicable.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** All category identifiers, academic semesters, and destination chips employ compact uppercase or tight sentence-case styling using `label-sm` or `label-md` with positive letter spacing (+0.02em to +0.04em) to preserve crisp legibility at small viewport sizes.
- **Display & Section Titles:** Emphasize geometric authority through negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) and tight line height ratios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column grid system calibrated for dynamic opportunity discovery:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 2rem gutters and generous 3rem page margins. Content max-width stops at 1440px to retain scan-ability across large displays. Filter sidebars occupy 3 columns (sticky) while opportunity card streams utilize 9 columns in a 3-column subgrid.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with 1.5rem gutters and 1.5rem margins. Reflows opportunity grids into 2-column card structures and transitions filter sidebars into collapsible drawer modals.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 1rem gutters and 1rem canvas margins. Visual cards convert to single-column vertical stacks. Horizontal snap-scroll carousels are leveraged for filter pills and destination chips.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (1.5rem). Tight metadata groups rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for unified association.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved without heavy, dirty drop shadows, maintaining an ethereal, light-filled aesthetic:

- **Canvas Ground:** Pure soft background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Level (Resting Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px hairline border in `#F1F5F9` and an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Level (Hovered Opportunity Cards / Floating Action Bars):** Slight translation of -3px vertically with expanded ambient diffusion: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`.
- **Overlay & Modal Level (Application Portals & Drawers):** High-diffusion elevation: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, backed by an ultra-soft frosted scrim (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.8)` with an 8px blur filter).

## Shapes

The design uses an inviting, modern pill-dominant geometry that softens the academic administrative experience:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** Standard 1rem corner radius (`rounded-2xl` equivalent).
- **Opportunity Cards & Modals:** Large, sweeping corner radii between 1.5rem (`rounded-2xl`) and 2rem (`rounded-3xl`) to establish friendly surfaces reminiscent of modern consumer travel applications.
- **Interactive Triggers & Indicators:** Full circle and full pill radiuses (9999px) for buttons, filter pills, academic match badges, and radial progress containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Sunset Coral):** Full pill shape (height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile). Solid fill `#F43F5E`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Hover: `#E11D48` with a subtle glow shadow `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Action (Sky Outline/Ghost):** Full pill shape. 1.5px border `#0284C7`, text `#0284C7`, background transparent. Hover: Background `#F0F9FF` (`primary-50`).
- **Tertiary/Auxiliary:** Ghost pill with slate text `#64748B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Filter Pills & Badges
- **Interactive Filter Pills:** Full pill contour (height: 36px), 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#334155`. Selected state: Background `#E0F2FE`, border `#0284C7`, text `#0369A1` with an inner dismiss icon.
- **Destination & Flag Badges:** Compact pills (height: 28px) displaying circular flag icons (16px) followed by country or university code. Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#E2E8F0`.

### Opportunity Exchange Cards
- **Geometry & Hierarchy:** White `#FFFFFF` card body with `24px` (1.5rem) border radius, enclosed by a 1px `#F1F5F9` edge.
- **Header:** Features university logo in a 44px rounded-xl container, accompanied by the institution name and destination city/country.
- **Radial Compatibility Score:** Positioned top-right. A 48px dual-ring SVG meter: muted track in `#E2E8F0` and active arc in `#10B981` (for scores >80%) or `#0284C7`. The numeric score (e.g., "94%") sits centered in bold `label-sm`.
- **Meta Chips Strip:** Horizontal stack of tags specifying funding type (e.g., "Full Erasmus+", "Tuition Waiver"), semester period, and remaining days until deadline.
- **Footer Section:** Subtly differentiated with `#FAFAFA` background, presenting housing stipend estimates and a direct "Ver Edital" CTA.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Search Bar:** Prominent floating 56px height pill with `#FFFFFF` background, inset search icon in `#0284C7`, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`, and an integrated right-aligned coral search button.
- **Form Inputs:** 48px height, 16px radius (`rounded-2xl`), border 1.5px solid `#E2E8F0`. Focus state transforms border to `#0284C7` with a 4px soft outer glow (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`).

### Academic Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px rounded squares (6px radius), 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#0284C7` featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** 20px concentric circles with a smooth inward transition ring when activated.
